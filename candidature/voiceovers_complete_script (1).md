## 💡 Production & Recording Pro-Tips
1.  **Quiet Environment:** Record in a room with soft fabrics (bed, curtains, rug) to absorb echo.
2.  **Mic Distance:** Keep your microphone (or phone mic) about 10-15 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side (to avoid "pop" breathing sounds).
3.  **Smile While Speaking:** Smiling physically changes your vocal cords, making your voice sound warmer, more positive, and confident!
4.  **Marque the Pauses:** Speak slowly. Take a 1 to 2-second breath at every ellipsis (`...`). It projects authority and expert calm.
